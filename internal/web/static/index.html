MKTPREDICT

An experiment, for fun. Not financial advice, and not to be used for any financial benefit. The model can be — and often is — wrong.

▸

    Every reading is produced by a fitted model — an OLS trend, a GARCH(1,1) volatility forecast, an implied-volatility surface and 20,000 simulated price paths — from public market data. No opinions, no news, no forecasts from anyone.

    Published figures are computed when the site is built, not live.
